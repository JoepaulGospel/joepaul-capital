How The Numbers Work
Credited toward goal = realized trading profit + confirmed prop payouts (deposits excluded — your own money is not profit). Configurable in Settings.
Remaining = max(0, target − credited).   Progress % = credited ÷ target × 100 (bar capped at 100%).
Return on capital = realized profit ÷ deposited capital — a simple measure, not money- or time-weighted.   Drawdown = (peak equity − current equity) ÷ peak equity — your personal peak-to-current measure, unrelated to any prop-firm daily loss limit.
